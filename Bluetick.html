About My Bluetick

Blueticks are easy to spot — that dark blue, mottled coat with black spots, long velvety ears, and soulful eyes. Mine is still a hound at heart.

Breed Quick Facts

Bluetick Coonhounds were bred for tracking and trailing. They are known for their cold nose, stamina, and that classic baying howl. Mine uses his nose for everything — even finding snacks I thought I hid well.

Personality

He's a perfect mix of stubborn hound and total softie.

What Makes Blueticks Special

On a walk, his nose is always on the ground. At home, he's glued to my side. He howls at sirens, sleeps like he ran a marathon, and thinks every visitor came just to pet him. Super smart, but only listens when he wants to — classic coonhound.

Favorite Things

If it involves sniffing or howling, he's in:

  • Long sniff walks where he can track everything
  • Howling along with sirens and coyotes
  • Tug toys and anything that squeaks
  • Riding with his head out the window, ears flapping
  • Curling up on the couch after a big adventure
